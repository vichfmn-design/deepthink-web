Privacy

Simpthink · effective November 13, 2026 · Version française

Simpthink collects no data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

What you write

Your hives, cells, topics, ideas and answers are stored only on your device. They are never sent to any server, ours or anyone else's.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, this data is included in that backup, like any other app's data. The backup is handled by Apple or by you, not by Simpthink.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact us through the app's App Store page.
